About Our UGC Creator Network

Welcome to IDYLL Productions.

We are a video editing agency that is now expanding into UGC (User-Generated Content) campaigns. We already work with brands, and more partnerships are being added regularly.

To make the collaboration process faster and more efficient, we're building a network of talented creators. Instead of searching for creators every time a brand needs a campaign, we'll already have your profile and details on file. This allows us to quickly find the best match for each project and contact you directly whenever a suitable opportunity becomes available.

Your information is not shared with brands or third parties without a valid business reason related to a collaboration. We take your privacy seriously, and all submitted information is stored securely using appropriate security measures. Your data is only used to evaluate your suitability for future campaigns and to contact you about relevant opportunities.

Please note that submitting your information does not guarantee campaign selection. Every brand has different requirements, and creators are chosen based on the campaign's specific needs. If you're a good fit for a project, we'll reach out to you using the contact details you provide.

Thank you for taking the time to join our creator network. We appreciate your interest in working with IDYLL Productions, and we look forward to the opportunity to collaborate with you in the future.
